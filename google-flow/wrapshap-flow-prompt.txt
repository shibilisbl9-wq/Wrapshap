FINAL vs REF (most important rule)
Our plan says every proof is real: real tests, real timers, real customers, our real kiosk. So:
- FINAL = graphics, art and mood shots that make no product claim. These can be posted.
- REF = a shot reference for our camera team. It is NOT for posting. Anything that shows a test result, the machine cutting, a timer, a real customer, or our kiosk is REF.
- Make REF assets as storyboard images (not videos) to save credits. Put a small "REF – NOT FOR POSTING" tag in the top corner of every REF image.

WHAT WRAPSHAP IS (use only these facts, never invent others)
- A kiosk that cuts a screen-protector film for your exact phone model in under 60 seconds. Thousands of models.
- The films are TPU and EPU. They are not glass.
- Four finishes, always shown in this order: CLEAR, PRIVACY, MATTE, TUFF.
- CLEAR: virtually invisible, 99%+ light transmittance.
- PRIVACY: blocks side views at 35°.
- MATTE: anti-glare micro-texture and an oil-repellent top layer. Less glare, fewer prints.
- TUFF: 0.25–0.35 mm, 4H–5H, an EPU layer that spreads the hit. TUFF is the only finish that may be linked to drops or impact.
- Say "scratch-resistant". Never write or show "scratch-proof", "unbreakable" or "shatterproof".
- Brand line: "No more almost." Sign-off: "Don't worry, we got you."

LOGO RULES
- Use the attached logo exactly as it is: same shape, same orange-to-amber gradient, same white outline. Never redraw, recolour, stretch, tilt or crop it.
- Only place the logo where this brief asks for it, large and flat on a plain area. Never inside video clips.
- Never draw six-pointed stars anywhere: not in the logo, not in doodles, not as sparkles. Sparkles are always four-pointed.

LOOK AND FEEL ("Yellow Edition")
- Colours: Wrap Yellow #FFD60A (main background), Ink #141311 (text and doodles), Paper #F3F2EE (cards and light backgrounds), Orange #F39200 (small accents). Amber #ECBF30 appears in the logo only.
- Type in images: big headlines in a tall, condensed, heavy sans (like Anton); punchy lines in black hand-written marker (like Permanent Marker); small text in a clean geometric sans (like DM Sans).
- Graphic motifs: black hand-drawn marker doodles (a crown, a smiley, four-point sparkles, curved arrows, loose circles around flaws, a thick marker strike-through over the word "almost"), white rough-edged label cards, sticker cut-outs with a white border and a soft shadow, a light paper grain.
- Photography: bright, punchy and natural. Everyday life in Lahore and Karachi. Pakistani people of mixed ages in everyday clothes (shalwar kameez, kurtas, jeans, some women in a dupatta or hijab). Real skin texture, natural hands with five fingers, no plastic AI look, no poverty clichés.
- Phones: generic modern smartphones with no visible brand logos. Screens are off, or show a plain abstract wallpaper. No real apps, no readable chats.
- Vertical posts: keep the top 12% and the bottom 20% free of text and logos (TikTok and Reels buttons sit there).

ALWAYS / NEVER
- Videos: no on-screen text, no captions, no subtitles, no music, no speech unless asked. Ambient sound only. We add text and trending audio in the edit.
- Images: include only the exact text given in quotes. English only. Spell it exactly; check every letter before saving.
- Never show a phone hitting the ground, cracking or breaking.
- No other brands or logos, and nothing that looks like Google Translate or any other app's interface.
